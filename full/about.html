Our Story

Cooking with the seasons since 2016

Saffron & Sage began as a twelve-table room with one wood oven and a simple idea: cook what is fresh, and treat every guest like family.

From Our Kitchen

Meet Elena Marchetti, our head chef

Elena trained in family kitchens before opening Saffron & Sage with her brother. Her cooking is simple on purpose: a few excellent ingredients, a real fire, and no shortcuts.

Every morning starts the same way — bread in the oven, stock on the stove, and a walk through the market to see what is best that day.

What We Believe

Three promises, kept every day

Seasonal first

Our menu follows the market. When an ingredient is out of season, it leaves the menu until it is good again.

Made from scratch

Pasta rolled by hand, bread baked each morning, desserts finished in our own kitchen — nothing arrives pre-made.

Everyone is welcome

Families, first dates, celebrations, and quiet solo dinners all get the same warm table and the same care.

Come and Taste

We would love to cook for you

Walk-ins are welcome at lunch. For dinner, especially Friday and Saturday, we recommend booking ahead.
