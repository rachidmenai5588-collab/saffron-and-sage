What you get

Everything a restaurant site needs

  • Home — hero, welcome section, signature dishes, opening hours, guest quotes
  • Menu — 16 dishes in 4 courses with a working category filter
  • Reservations — reservation request form with front-end validation
  • Gallery — photo gallery with a JavaScript lightbox (click, arrow keys, Esc)
  • About — story, head chef, and house promises
  • Contact — address, hours, and a message form

One shared stylesheet and one script power all six pages, with a shared header (including a mobile menu), navigation, and footer. Responsive and mobile-first: single column on phones, two columns from 768px, three columns from 1200px.

Easy to make your own

Change the restaurant name, address, phone, email, and hours with a simple search-and-replace. Recolor the whole site by editing four variables at the top of the stylesheet. Swap the fonts by changing one link in each page head. Menu dishes and gallery photos are single blocks you can copy, edit, or remove.

Please note: the reservation and contact forms are front-end only — they validate input and show a confirmation message, but they do not send email or save bookings. Connect them to your own booking system or form service before going live. Demo photographs come from Unsplash for preview; for a production site, host your own photography or verify the license terms of any photo you keep.
